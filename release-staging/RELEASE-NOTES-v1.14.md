# LinkGuard v1.14 (versionCode 15)

## Fresh new look — "Bold Blocks" redesign
A full visual refresh across the whole app for a cleaner, more modern feel:

- **Home** — new gradient status card, bold colour stat tiles, blue primary actions.
- **Scan History** — colour-coded result rows with clear status pills and risk scores.
- **Scan Report** — solid colour verdict card (danger / suspicious / safe) at a glance.
- **Block screen & Threat Alert** — clearer, higher-contrast danger warnings.
- **QR scanner & update screens** — restyled to match.
- Status headline now honestly reflects whether protection is on or off.

No changes to scanning, detection, or your saved history — purely a visual update.
